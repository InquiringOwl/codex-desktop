Lab **C+graph**: two editable functions from a small family (linear, quadratic, √, 1/x); mode *Combine*: graphs of f, g and f+g or f·g (pointwise sum shown as vertical segments at a draggable x); mode *Compose*: arrow diagram x → g(x) → f(g(x)) on the graphs (drag x), readout domain of f∘g vs g∘f; mode *Steps*: evaluate (f∘g)(a) and simplify f(g(x)) step by step (guarded). Keys: f amber c1, g cyan c2, result pink c3, domain violet c4.
**a2-transformations** Transformations of Functions — pre a1-quad-graphs, a1-piecewise, g-transformations — unlocks a2-quad-vertex, a2-poly-graphs, a2-rational-func, a2-radical-func, a2-exp-func. g(x) = a·f(b(x − h)) + k: shifts, reflections (a < 0, b < 0), vertical/horizontal stretch and compression, order of operations on points (x, y) → (x/b + h, ay + k); parent functions x, x², x³, |x|, √x, ∛x, 1/x. Lab **A**: parent select + sliders a, b, h, k; ghost parent dashed; 3 key points mapped with arrows; *Explore* / *Match* (target curve, guarded parameters) / *Order* (apply moves one at a time with a stepper, showing why horizontal moves act "backwards"). Keys: h amber c1, k cyan c2, a pink c3, b violet c4, parent green c5.
**a2-inverses** Inverse Functions — pre a2-func-ops, a1-literal — unlocks a2-radical-func, a2-logs. One-to-one, horizontal line test, f⁻¹ by swapping and solving, f(f⁻¹(x)) = x both ways, domain/range swap, reflection in y = x, restricting a domain (x², x ≥ 0), f⁻¹ ≠ 1/f. Lab **D**: function family select; drag a point on f, its mirror on f⁻¹ across y = x with a dashed perpendicular; horizontal line test sweep (drag a horizontal line; count hits; restrict-domain toggle); *Steps* mode: find f⁻¹ algebraically (guarded). Keys: f amber c1, f⁻¹ cyan c2, y = x violet c4, test line pink c3.
**a2-quad-vertex** Quadratic Functions in Vertex Form — pre a2-transformations, a1-quad-sqrt — unlocks a2-parabolas. f(x) = a(x − h)² + k from y = ax² + bx + c by completing the square (incl. a ≠ 1, fractions), h = −b/(2a), max/min value, range, writing a quadratic from vertex + point, optimisation (area, revenue). Lab **A+C**: sliders a, b, c; *Graph*: vertex, axis, range bracket, both forms live; *Complete the square*: stepper over algebra-tile-style square plus steps panel (guarded vertex); *Fit*: drag vertex and one point, readout a. Keys: a pink c3, h amber c1, k cyan c2, the square/added term violet c4.
**a2-quad-form-eq** Equations in Quadratic Form — pre a1-quad-factor, a1-quad-formula — unlocks none. u-substitution: x⁴ − 5x² + 4 = 0, (x − 2)² + 3(x − 2) − 10 = 0, x − 7√x + 10 = 0, x^(2/3) + x^(1/3) − 6 = 0, x⁻² terms; back-substitute, reject u-values that give no real x (u = x² < 0) and check radicals. Lab **C**: problem picker; two linked panels u-world (parabola in u, its roots) and x-world (quartic or radical graph, roots), stepper reveals substitution → solve in u → back-substitute (guarded); a negative u-root visibly gives no x-intercept. Keys: u amber c1, x cyan c2, rejected pink c3.
**a2-sys-three** Linear Systems in Three Variables — pre a1-sys-elim — unlocks none. Ordered triples, elimination to a 2 × 2 system, back-substitution, inconsistent and dependent cases (parametric answer), applications (mixtures, coin/ticket, curve through 3 points y = ax² + bx + c). No matrices. Lab **C**: stepper over elimination with the three equations as rows (highlight the pair combined), plus a simple 3D view (rotatable oblique projection of three planes' traces or a cube with the solution point) or, if 3D is unclear, the parabola-through-3-points application graph; *Cases* mode shows one/none/infinitely many. Keys: eq1 amber c1, eq2 cyan c2, eq3 pink c3, solution green c5.

### Era 7 · Conic Sections
**a2-conic-sections** Conic Sections & the General Equation — pre g-circle-equations, a1-quad-sqrt — unlocks a2-parabolas, a2-ellipses, a2-nonlinear-sys. Conics as slices of a double cone (circle, ellipse, parabola, hyperbola, degenerate cases), the general equation Ax² + Cy² + Dx + Ey + F = 0 (no xy term), classifying by A and C, completing the square to standard form, circle review only as the first case. Lab **D**: *Slice*: a 2D side view of a double cone with a draggable cutting line (angle vs the cone's slant decides the type) plus the resulting curve; *Classify*: sliders A, C, D, E, F with `P.implicit` and `conic()` readout (type, centre/vertex); *Standard form* stepper (guarded). Keys: plane/slice amber c1, curve cyan c2, centre/vertex pink c3, coefficients violet c4.
**a2-parabolas** Parabolas: Focus & Directrix — pre a2-conic-sections, a2-quad-vertex — unlocks none. Locus definition (equal distance to focus and directrix), (x − h)² = 4p(y − k) and (y − k)² = 4p(x − h), p and opening, latus rectum 4p, reflective property (satellite dish), converting from general form. Lab **D**: drag the focus and directrix; a draggable point on the parabola shows the two equal distances; *Reflect* mode: parallel rays reflect to the focus. Keys: focus pink c3, directrix violet c4, p amber c1, distances cyan c2.
**a2-ellipses** Ellipses — pre a2-conic-sections — unlocks a2-hyperbolas. Locus (sum of distances = 2a), (x − h)²/a² + (y − k)²/b² = 1 with a > b, c² = a² − b², vertices, co-vertices, foci, eccentricity c/a, horizontal vs vertical, orbits (Kepler, whispering galleries). Lab **D**: drag foci and the string length 2a (string-and-pins drawing animates); point on ellipse with d₁ + d₂ readout; sliders for a, b; e readout. Keys: a amber c1, b cyan c2, c/foci pink c3, e violet c4.
**a2-hyperbolas** Hyperbolas — pre a2-ellipses — unlocks none. Locus (|d₁ − d₂| = 2a), x²/a² − y²/b² = 1 and vertical form, c² = a² + b², asymptotes y − k = ±(b/a)(x − h), fundamental rectangle, foci, eccentricity > 1, applications (LORAN/navigation, cooling towers). Lab **D**: sliders a, b, h, k, orientation; fundamental rectangle and asymptotes drawn first, then branches (`P.implicit`); draggable point with |d₁ − d₂|; compare with ellipse toggle. Keys: a amber c1, b cyan c2, c/foci pink c3, asymptotes violet c4.
**a2-nonlinear-sys** Nonlinear Systems of Equations — pre a2-conic-sections, a1-sys-sub — unlocks none. Line–conic and conic–conic systems, substitution and elimination, 0/1/2/3/4 intersection points, real solutions only (complex intersections noted), applications (dimensions from area and perimeter). Lab **D**: choose a conic (circle/ellipse/hyperbola/parabola) and a line or second conic with sliders; intersections found (`intersect`/implicit) and labelled; count readout; *Solve* mode steps through substitution (guarded). Keys: first curve amber c1, second cyan c2, intersections green c5.
